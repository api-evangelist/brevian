# Brevian

> Brevian is a Revenue Execution AI platform for sales teams. It prepares reps before calls, guides them in real time during calls, captures deal context automatically into the CRM, and detects patterns across deals — turning an organization's collective product and deal knowledge into real-time, actionable guidance. Founded by Vinay Wagh (CEO, ex-Databricks) and Anupreet Walia (CTO); backed by Felicis.

This llms.txt was GENERATED by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public Brevian surface (Brevian does not publish its own /llms.txt).
